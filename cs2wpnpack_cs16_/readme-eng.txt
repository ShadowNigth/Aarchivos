CS2 Weapon Pack for CS 1.6

This pack includes the main weapons from Counter-Strike 2, adapted for Counter-Strike 1.6. The models are multi-rig, meaning that any weapon is compatible with the hands and arms available in the pack. Knives and PW models have not been added yet and textures have been optimized for better looks.
